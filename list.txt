! explicit pages (largely removing “Explore” in places it shouldn’t be)

! Hide globally generated feeds for ‘trending’ content or MS GitHub asserting
! itself into feeds.

! Hide extra popularity contest (‘stars’) & other places that pressure users
! into making more contributions on this platform. Free software relies on
! contributions, but gamification or inducing anxiety on one’s amount of
! contributions can often be harmful — especially if we consider the pressure
! to push towards permissive licenses that the corporations encourage but
! rarely donate back to with support or finances.
github.com##.js-yearly-contributions
github.com##.Layout-sidebar .js-profile-editable-replace div:has(div > a[href$="tab=achievements"])
github.com##a[href*="tab=stars"]:has([data-component^="ActionList.Item"])

! Hide extra pressures to ‘sponsor’. Users can already find this info on the
! primary page & Microsoft takes a cut of sponsorships; prefer donating on
! another platform when possible anyhow.
github.com###issuecomment-new div:has(#new_comment_form) ~ .Box:last-child
github.com##[data-testid="search-sub-header"] ~ [class^="Box-"] a[href^="/sponsors"]:upward([class^="Box-"])
